Restoration and Customization Services for Two-Wheelers

At Servall, we believe your two-wheeler should be as unique as you are. Whether you’re looking to restore a classic bike to its former glory or customize your current ride to reflect your personality, our expert services are designed to transform your vehicle into a masterpiece. With our dedication to quality and craftsmanship, we provide endless possibilities to make your ride truly one-of-a-kind.

Our Restoration Services

Complete Overhaul: We meticulously restore vintage and classic two-wheelers, ensuring every component is repaired or replaced to meet original specifications.

Engine Rebuilding: Our skilled technicians rebuild engines to ensure they run as smoothly and efficiently as new, preserving the heart of your beloved vehicle.

Paint and Bodywork: Using advanced paint-matching technology and high-quality materials, we restore your bike’s original appearance or give it a new look while retaining its classic charm.

Parts Sourcing: We source rare and hard-to-find parts to maintain the authenticity of your vintage or classic two-wheeler.

Frame Restoration: Our precision frame restoration services ensure the structural integrity and alignment of your vehicle, providing a safe and enjoyable ride.

Our Customization Services

Aesthetic Enhancements: From custom paint jobs and decals to unique body kits and accessories, we offer a range of aesthetic enhancements to make your two-wheeler stand out.

Performance Upgrades: Enhance your ride’s performance with our specialised upgrades, including exhaust systems, suspension modifications, and engine tuning. We ensure all modifications comply with government norms and regulations for safety and emissions.

Comfort and Convenience: Improve your riding experience with custom seats, ergonomic handlebars, advanced lighting systems, and other comfort-oriented modifications.

Technology Integration: Integrate modern technology with features like GPS navigation, advanced security systems, and smartphone connectivity for a smarter ride.

Why Choose Servall for Restoration and Customization?

Expert Craftsmanship: Our team of skilled technicians and designers are experts in their field, ensuring that every restoration and customization is executed with precision and care.

Quality Assurance: We use only the highest quality materials and components, ensuring that your restorations and customizations are durable and reliable.

Personalised Service: We work closely with you to understand your vision and preferences, delivering results that exceed your expectations.

Comprehensive Solutions: Whether you’re looking for minor tweaks or a complete overhaul, we provide comprehensive solutions tailored to your needs.

Compliance with Government Norms: We ensure that all performance enhancements comply with relevant government regulations for safety and emissions, guaranteeing that your vehicle remains road-legal.

Our Process

Consultation: We begin with a detailed consultation to understand your ideas, preferences, and requirements.

Approval: We review the plan with you, making any necessary adjustments to ensure it aligns perfectly with your vision.

Execution: Our expert technicians bring the design to life, using the best tools and techniques.

Quality Check: Each project undergoes rigorous quality checks to ensure it meets our high standards.

Delivery: We present your restored or customized two-wheeler, ready for you to ride in style and confidence.

Transform your two-wheeler into a reflection of your personality with Servall’s restoration and customization services. Whether you’re looking to bring a classic bike back to life or add personal touches to your current ride, we have the expertise and passion to bring your vision to life. Experience the difference of a truly personalised ride with Servall.
