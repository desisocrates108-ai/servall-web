Frequently asked questions

What is Servall?
Servall is a Multi Brand 2 Wheeler Workshop Chain. It has started in 2015 & Now providing It’s services & Unique User Experience through It’s Franchise Network.
Which Vehicles are you servicing right now?
We are One Stop Solution for all your 2 Wheeler Needs. We are providing services to Every Brand, Every Model, Every Variant for Every 2 Wheeler Need whether it’s Ev or Petrol Operated 2 Wheeler.
How are your services different from other Garages?

There is a huge difference between a Servall Workshop & Local Garages.

  • We are fully equipped with the latest tools & equipment for quality repairs.
  • We are focused on periodic maintenance to keep your vehicle ever ready.
  • We never ignore any vehicle or any customer.
  • We maintain your vehicle history.
  • We provide customer-centric services such as a customer service booking app, pick-up & drop, breakdown assistance, service reminders, and also help with insurance services.
  • We assist customers with warranty claims for batteries & tires.
  • We are focused on after-sales service.
  • We have cultivated a culture of transparency in billing.
What are additional Benefits if I choose your Services?
  • Pick up & Drop
  • Breakdown Assistance
  • Access to wide Workshop Network
  • Maintenance History Keep Up
  • Service Booking App
  • Service Reminders
  • AMC & Some Frequent Value for Money offers
  • Clean & Tidy Customer Lounge
  • Service Advisors to Record your Problems
  • CRE for Assurance of your Mentioned Problems
Are your Service Charges Expensive?
No, We are not overpricing neither spare nor Labour. We have fixed our Labour chart & It’s according to Industry Standards. Besides, We have some service Packages which can be availed to get more cost efficient services.
What is Quality of Material, you are using in your Workshop?
We are using the best quality of Spares & Lubes to maintain our Repair Quality & Customer Experience. We are using all genuine spares, OEM Quality Spares & Performax Spares. Performax Spares is the Brand under Our Umbrella. We are having contract manufacturing with industry leader manufacturers to give our customers best quality at affordable rates.
