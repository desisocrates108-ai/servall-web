Frequently Asked Questions

What is Servall Franchise?
Servall is a Multi-Brand 2 Wheeler Workshop Franchise based on the FOFO (Franchise Owned Franchise Operated) Model.
What are the benefits of owning a multi-brand 2 wheeler workshop franchise with your company?
  • Established brand name and reputation
  • Proven business model for profitability
  • Access to training and support systems
  • Multi-brand servicing capability to attract a wider customer range
  • Less risk compared to a self-initiated business
How many franchise outlets do you currently have?
We currently have 26 franchise outlets across India.
What is the initial investment required to open a franchise?
We have 3 Different Models to invest in Franchise. You can choose according to your Area & Budget. Investments starts From 5 Lakhs to 15 Lakhs for Different Models.Please contact us for detailed investment information. We can provide a breakdown of franchise fees, set-up costs, and ongoing royalties.
What are the eligibility criteria to become a franchisee?
We typically look for individuals with:
  • Entrepreneurial spirit and business acumen
  • Strong leadership and management skills
  • Financial viability
  • Passion for the two-wheeler industry
  • Ability to comply with brand standards and operating procedures
Do I need prior experience in the two-wheeler repair industry?
Prior experience is an advantage, but not mandatory. We provide comprehensive training programs to equip you with the necessary knowledge and skills.
Where can I open a franchise outlet?
We have 3 Types of Model to Comply area constrained. It can be anywhere in India.
Can I choose the location of my franchise outlet?
We may provide recommendations based on market research and demographic data. However, final approval will depend on mutual agreement.
What kind of training and support do you offer to franchisees?
We offer a comprehensive training program covering:
  • Workshop operations and management
  • Technical skills for implementing SOPs in repair & maintenance
  • Customer service best practices
  • Marketing and sales strategies
Ongoing support includes technical assistance, CR Support , Spare Procurement and marketing resources.
How do I inquire about franchise opportunities?
Contact us directly to schedule a consultation.
What is the typical return on investment (ROI) for your franchise model?
While we cannot guarantee specific ROI, we can share historical data and projected growth figures to help you understand the potential profitability.
What are the ongoing fees associated with the franchise agreement?
We typically charge Fixed royalties. We will provide details in the franchise disclosure document.
